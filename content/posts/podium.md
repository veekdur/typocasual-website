This is the last frame on the sheet and it is the only one that fails as a photograph of
architecture, because there is no architecture left in it to photograph.

Standing there, the thing you notice is not the absence of the building. It is the absence of
any evidence that the absence is recent. The ground has closed over the pad. The grass is not
colonising; it is established, and has been for years.

## What the sheet is actually recording

Read left to right, the sheet goes from a structure that is unmistakably a structure to this.
Nothing in between is a surprise. Frame 001 is bare concrete; frame 020 is a field with a
slope in it that might be a ramp.

The slope is a ramp. That is the only tell.

> You would not know there was a building here, which is a different thing from saying there
> is no evidence. There is evidence. It is just a shape in the ground.

## Why keep the frame at all

Because a contact sheet that only shows the flattering frames is not a contact sheet. The
sheet is supposed to be the whole roll, printed at one size, including the frames where the
subject has left.

Everything before it is the argument. This frame is the conclusion, and it is quiet, and it
is greener than anything else on the page.
