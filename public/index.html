typocasual — spelled wrong on purpose

a structure, left out in the weather

Every frame is printed at the same size. The sheet does not rank the work — it lays it out, and lets the weather in.

Twelve plates, four typefaces, four cards. Read left to right and the structure gives way: the first frames are bare concrete, the last are barely legible as architecture.

Concrete was an argument about permanence. Moss is a shorter argument, and it is winning.
